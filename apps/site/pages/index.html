Shuttle runs coding agents in a sandbox on your own computer. When you have to leave, it carries the session to the cloud, and brings it back when you sit down again.

Working on your Mac, in a virtual machine of its own. Send it across.

On your Mac

Each session works in a small Linux virtual machine that ships inside the app. It has its own kernel and can't see your files. There is nothing else to install, Docker included.

In between

One click moves a session across. Its branches and uncommitted files go with it, and the agents pick up where they stopped. It usually takes a few seconds.

In the cloud

The session keeps working with your laptop shut, and teammates can join it. Bring it home when you're back at your desk.

Shuttle works with Claude Code, Codex and Cursor, signed in with your own subscriptions, and opens pull requests on the GitHub repositories you connect.

Build your own ADE from Shuttle's parts.

Everything in the app is a React component you can install from npm as @swirls/shuttle: the session, the conversation, the composer, the artifacts pane. Lay them out your way, put your own parts beside them, and add your own kinds of tab.
