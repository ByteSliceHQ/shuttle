The engine a host runs Shuttle's sessions on is now in the package: `@swirls/shuttle/engine` (host ports and headless sessions), `@swirls/shuttle/engine/worker` (the Durable Objects a host's Worker binds), and the modules under `@swirls/shuttle/engine/*`, `@swirls/shuttle/git/*` and `@swirls/shuttle/sandbox/*`. See `docs/hosting.md`.
